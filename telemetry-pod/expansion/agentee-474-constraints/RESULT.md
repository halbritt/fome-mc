## How to read the register

Coordinates are board XY in mm, viewed from the front, with +Y toward the bottom. `F` and `B` mean the component faces. Bounding boxes are `[xmin,ymin,xmax,ymax]` of native courtyard polygons, not toleranced collision solids. Full polygons, exact pad/drill geometry, UUIDs and current allocation membership are in `native-geometry.json`; `geometry-summary.json` is the smaller anchor/test-access view. No 3D collision result is claimed.

Classes: **OWNER** = recorded owner requirement/quote; **OWNER-RELAY** = explicitly labelled agent relay, not owner-authored text; **MFR** = retained manufacturer drawing/catalog or published capability; **MECH-DERIVED** = geometry derived from a cited drawing or actual CAD; **AGENT** = chosen default, margin, historical pose or engineering policy. A mixed row labels the individual parts rather than promoting its whole contents to OWNER/MFR. **UNKNOWN** is not a zero dimension or a passed check.

`P` below means material to accepting a mechanically constrained packing on the affected region/face. `R` means installed/final-release qualification. A `P` unknown can remain explicit in a provisional exploratory candidate; it prevents claiming that particular requirement passed. It does not invent a hard exclusion or stop all generation.
