Decision

J1 TE AMPSEAL 1-776163-1 is a through-hole soldered header with a mechanical
retention pin. Its leads may protrude several mm. Assembly: seat the
connector, tack one pin, re-seat/align, solder the rest, trim the excess lead
stickout.

The standard JLCPCB four-layer, nominal 1.6 mm board is ACCEPTED as the
board choice, with JLCPCB's published +/-10% finished-thickness tolerance
(1.44-1.76 mm). That fabricator tolerance is a BOARD-SIDE fact only; it is
not the basis for J1 fit.

Component-side fit rule (owner clarification, Cairn 9561cd0c): for ordinary
soldered through-hole parts the owner uses AT LEAST +/-10% of the
manufacturer's recommended nominal board thickness as an inclusive
fit-screening default (often wider). This is independent of, and must not
be conflated with, the fabricator's finished-thickness tolerance. For J1,
TE's recommended nominal 1.57 mm gives a screening band of 1.413-1.727 mm;
the 1.6 mm nominal board is inside it (about 1.9% from nominal), so J1
screens as fitting. Explicit manufacturer functional limits override the
default; being outside the heuristic band alone is advisory, not a defect.
The reusable agent-ee policy is AGENTEE-448.

The TE 1.57 mm recommendation (0.03 mm nominal difference) is NOT a thickness
gate, and interval arithmetic is NOT an actionable release uncertainty.

No special tolerance, TE confirmation, enclosure CAD or owner harness is
required for placement, routing or bare-board fab readiness.

Normal footprint, finished-hole/pad, retention and solder-joint checks remain.

A narrower requirement needs a concrete source or an observed failure.

Related owner direction (same day, same relay)

The ECU and telemetry pod are built from the ground up. The enclosure, panel
and harness are team design deliverables; no CAD exists yet, and that is not a
blocker. The only existing external constraint is the large seat cowl.
Installed fit against the seat cowl and the designed enclosure/harness is
