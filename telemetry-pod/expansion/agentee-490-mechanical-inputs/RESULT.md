# AGENTEE-490 bounded mechanical input result

The standard JLCPCB thickness range cannot guarantee insertion into the selected Molex J2 slot. `DECISIONS.md` is the single decision table; `J2-decision.json` gives the sourced interval arithmetic and concrete alternatives. The recommended finite alternative is a standard Amphenol 132134 vertical SMA jack, with a checked five-pad candidate at `(103,40,F0)`. It changes the BOM, footprint, access axis and RF launch, so agent-449 must route that choice through agent-519. It is not adopted here. Retaining the Molex instead needs the explicit tighter finished-edge requirement in option B, which is not a published JLCPCB standard capability.

The J2 underside bound also needs a seating datum. Under an upper-contact/F seating assumption, the largest slot plus largest lower leg minus the thinnest published board gives **2.4208 mm below B**. The earlier 2.1082 mm is lower-leg thickness, not a complete B-side assembled bound. Solder stand-off and native body registration remain separate terms.

J1 now has a finite drawing-based external mating arrangement: the selected plug family has a 39.69 mm axial maximum; the application specification recommends a Ø66 mm opening to clear the completed 35-way assembly. That clearance opening is separate from the published header sealing-panel cutout. Keep the plug and strain relief external to the local panel interface, or use the explicitly bounded internal approach alternative. Neither approach promotes a rejected CAD intersection to a qualified mating pose. Exact stop/contact/seal/latch registration remains missing.

For J3, the proposed 0.2 mm inward move to `(9.1,40,B90)` retains all 14 native pad boxes, no drills, and 0.775 mm nominal edge spacing against either plain or proposed-notched outline. The regular ±0.2 mm routing tolerance leaves 0.575 mm in the stated geometric arithmetic. Prefer bare-board B-side flat-blade access and no notch; the 1×4 mm notch stays a finite fallback pending review and actual card/tool Z evidence. The retained catalog already shows nominal 16.75/17.55/21.55 mm card positions. Its 1.68 mm socket height remains nominal, not a maximum.

`bound-reservations.json` carries the exact 486 UUID-bound hardware, iron and probe recipes plus the new conditional J2/J1/J3 recipes. It separates bare-board work from installed hardware/harness. Harwin's retained drawing confirms a through-threaded M3 female/female 12 mm spacer with length 11.7–12.3 mm and AF 5.3–5.7 mm. The proposed shorter floor screw has an explicit opposed-tip inequality; screw/floor tolerances and strength are still required. Nylon's flammability rating is not a service-temperature qualification. Exact antenna/SMA-variant, cable bend/torque, thermal rise and complete installed stack remain unresolved with named finite alternatives. No full enclosure is designed and roughly 90 mm HEIGHT remains an owner estimate.

Native controls ran on peecee-wsl through `native-admit` test/weight 1, KiCad 10.0.7, reading the immutable board. They loaded the alternative SMA footprint and moved J3 only in memory; no board was saved. The first serialization attempt failed on a KiCad UTF8 value, was corrected to a string, and the complete controls were rerun. These are pad/drill/edge geometry checks, not full-board DRC, packing, a solder-joint qualification or RF acceptance.

All work uses the supplied immutable 486 commit `51658d3`, 480 commit `b389b47a` and canonical source `b6830548`. The moving writer tree was not read. No production design, membership, pin relations, source rules, provenance or RF nominal was changed. The ordinary RF geometry stays 0.293/0.2032, with scoped launch exceptions reviewed on actual saved geometry. Both board-level rule areas and affected cutouts must regenerate after dependency changes. Agent-449 integrates and arranges the one Fable HIGH review.
