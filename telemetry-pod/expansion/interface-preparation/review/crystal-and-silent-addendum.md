# Crystal and silent-control candidate addendum

The proposed Epson X1E0003410031 / C725712 is suitable to carry forward as an explicitly unqualified crystal candidate. An unavailable active oscillator is not a reason to block native schematic preparation. No exact-part procurement or startup qualification is established here.

Captured Epson FA-238A family sheet C725712.pdf, page 1, establishes fundamental operation; -40 to +125 C; ESR maximum 60 ohm at 20 MHz (specified across that temperature range at 100 microwatt drive); drive maximum 200 microwatts with recommended 1–100 microwatts; family temperature deviation ±50 ppm over -40 to +125 C; first-year aging ±5 ppm at 25 C. Exact ordering-code frequency, CL=12 pF and ±15 ppm initial tolerance currently rely on the separately captured catalog identification. The family sheet alone does not bind every suffix option. Preserve that evidence distinction. The first-year simple sum 15+50+5=70 ppm excludes load pulling, later aging and operating-point changes; do not call ±15 ppm a total frequency budget.

Two equal 18 pF capacitors produce a series load of 9 pF. Adding an assumed total 3 pF parasitic load gives 12 pF. This is a reasonable initial C0G value, conditional on that definition of stray capacitance; do not add 3 pF independently at each node and still claim the same result. Include tolerances and pin/trace capacitance in refinement. Parent may implement 18 pF as provisional while explicitly retaining exact-part load, negative-resistance/startup margin and drive-level measurement across supply/temperature as release gates. MCP2518FD supports 20 MHz crystals but does not publish a 20 MHz minimum transconductance or universal allowable ESR; its 40 MHz guidance cannot qualify this combination.

Epson family pin arrangement: resonator between pads 1 and 3; case pads 2 and 4 grounded. Connect 1/3 to OSC1/OSC2, one load capacitor from each oscillator net to ground. Orientation of the electrical resonator ends is interchangeable; footprint pad numbering and ground cases still require native checking. No drive resistor value is justified solely by these documents; any drive-limiting resistor choice needs measured drive and startup trade-off.

Confirmed SOIC MCP pin map: 1 TXCAN, 2 RXCAN, 3 CLKO/SOF, 4 INT, 5 OSC2, 6 OSC1, 7 VSS, 8 GPIO1/INT1, 9 GPIO0/INT0/XSTBY, 10 SCK, 11 SDI, 12 SDO, 13 nCS, 14 VDD. T suffix is a packaging/order variant, not an extra reset pin; verify exact MPN footprint in emitted source.

TJA1051T/3 S pin 8 driven by MCP GPIO0 pin 9 with 10 kohm to shared 3.3 V is suitable for a startup-silent candidate. MCP IOCON POR has PM0=1, TRIS0=1, XSTBYEN=0: GPIO input. TJA S has an internal pulldown, but NXP Table 7 limits high input current to 10 microamps, so the external 10 kohm pull-up loses only 0.10 V from that load (plus MCP input leakage). This remains comfortably high on the proposed rail. Keep PM0=1 and XSTBYEN=0; do not enable automatic XSTBY control, whose semantics are different from S.

Firmware sequence: write LAT0=1 using a single-byte IOCON write while GPIO0 remains input; then configure TRIS0=0, preserving GPIO mode and unrelated bits. Keep S high while initializing clock, ECC RAM, bit timing, FIFOs and acceptance filters. Only then write LAT0=0 with a single-byte IOCON write to permit ACK/transmit. This avoids driving the reset LAT0=0 prematurely. INTOD affects interrupt outputs; it does not turn GPIO0 into open drain.

A shared-rail power-on or controller RESET returns GPIO0 to input and the resistor pulls S high. **An ESP-only supervisor reset does not reset the MCP2518FD**, so the controller can retain GPIO0 low and remain active during MCU restart. The circuit is startup-silent, not guaranteed silent on every MCU fault/reset. If the requirement becomes hardware-enforced silence during any MCU reset, this connection needs additional gating; do not represent firmware recovery as hardware enforcement. A silent transceiver cannot ACK frames, so enable normal mode for an otherwise unacknowledged sensor bus once initialization completes.

Source scope: read-only datasheet/circuit review; native delta checker is still required after emission. Existing sensor power, wheel conditioning, protection, hardware timing and physical qualification holds remain.
